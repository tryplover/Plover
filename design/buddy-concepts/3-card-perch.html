3 - Perched on its own task card

Rationale. The buddy's window is a small calm task card (current step, Pomodoro time, "2 of 5") and the bird sits on that card's top edge like a bird on a fence, front-facing, toes and wing tips curled over the lip. Because the card is always there, the bird gets a real, believable place to be: it never floats, it needs no desktop knowledge, and the card carries the useful information so the bird can stay tiny and quiet. Focus mode is the strongest move: the card collapses to a timer pill and the bird tucks down behind it, so the whole thing shrinks to almost nothing. The bird is a full round bird (cream body, sand cap, pointed wing tips, amber beak, two toes) with no collar band, and a thin sand outline so it reads on any background.

Honest weaknesses. The unit is tall (about 240 px with bubble headroom) and wider than a free bird, so it asks for more screen than the other ideas. Card and bird compete: the card text is the real content and the bird risks feeling like a hat on a widget. The perch is its own card only, so it can never interact with other windows. The bird is small (about 115 px) and its expressions rely on tiny eyes. Focus mode hides most of the character at the moment the user is most engaged, which is intentional but means little personality there. The transparent window needs click-through on the headroom area above the bird and bubble.
