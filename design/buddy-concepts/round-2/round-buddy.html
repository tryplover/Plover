Round buddy: corner sitter

The round peek bird, whole: one ball (head and body are the same circle), a short sand cap, amber beak, calm eyes, and the feather-tip wings pointing up at its sides so it can use them: wave, cheer, tuck. Tiny feet so it sits on the taskbar edge. No collar, no extra body parts. Motion stays small: breathing, blinks, a glance, a wave, a hop.
