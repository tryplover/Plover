Plover mascot: "Tide"

Concept 03, a calm, breathing, nearly faceless shorebird

Tide treats Plover like a wellness companion rather than a watcher. The bird has no face while it is awake, so it never seems to be staring at you. It reads through rhythm, warmth and soft light: a slow breath when idle, a steady pulse while you work, a warm amber bloom when a subtask moves, and a cool lavender shimmer for a nudge. The only time a facial feature appears is paused, when a closed-eye crescent, a tucked head, desaturated color and a flat, dotted shoreline make "not watching" unmistakable at 32px. The ripple line borrows the plover's shoreline, so progress feels like a tide coming in rather than a task ticking over.
