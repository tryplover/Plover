Pebble

Plover mascot concept 01. Geometric primitives, expression from the eyes and squash-and-stretch.

Pebble reduces the plover to one egg-shaped body, a small orange triangle beak and dot eyes, so it still reads as a bird at 32px in the overlay. All the personality comes from motion: it squashes before a hop and stretches at the top, its eyes glance around or squint while you focus, and they curve into happy arcs when a subtask moves forward. The plover cues are the black breast collar and the stick legs, and they are added in steps across the three variants so you can see how much detail the small size can carry. Privacy has one clear signal: closed eyes, a desaturated body and stillness mean Plover is not watching. Nudges are a single curious head-tilt and a soft amber dot, with no shaking and no red.
