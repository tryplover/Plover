Plover mascot concepts · 02

Glyph: a brand mark that becomes a bird

Glyph treats Plover's character as an animated logo rather than a cartoon: two to five strokes or petals that settle into a plover silhouette (round head, wedge beak, collar) when at rest and fold into an abstract asterisk while Plover is working. That fold between "bird" and "mark" is the status signal, so you can read the state at 32px from overall shape instead of small details. The plover's black breast-band does double duty as Plover's progress ring. It is the only part that changes color: deep brown at rest, sea-glass when progress lands, dune-amber for a nudge, grey when paused. Paused is unambiguous: the eye closes to a lid line, the head tucks, the mark goes grey, and in the line variant the outline breaks into a dashed "off" line. Motion borrows the real bird's run-and-stop rhythm. It moves in short bursts with long still rests, so it never feels like it is staring at you.

A · Single line

  • One continuous outline (tail, back, crown, beak, breast, belly) plus a collar stroke and an eye dot, drawn with stroke-dasharray.
  • Working: a sea-glass "pen" comet traces the outline in a loop. Progress: the whole outline redraws in sea-glass, then a hop and three spark ticks.
  • Paused: the line breaks into dashes and goes grey, and the eye closes to a lid. Pure CSS, the lightest option to ship in the overlay.

B · Stroke cluster

  • Five open strokes (back, belly, collar, head arc, beak chevron) that together read as the plover, morphed point-for-point in JS.
  • Working: the strokes straighten into a five-spoke asterisk that turns slowly, with the eye as its center dot ("still looking, thinking").
  • Progress bursts into the asterisk and snaps back to the bird with a hop. Nudge: the bird stands tall twice with an amber collar. Paused: grey, tucked, eyes shut.

C · Filled petals

  • Four filled petals (body teardrop, collar crescent, round head, beak wedge) and a face-colored eye. The most logo-like and the boldest at 32px.
  • Working: the petals bloom into a four-petal rosette that rotates and breathes slowly, so the mark looks like it is mulling things over.
  • The collar petal carries every state color. Paused flattens the whole mark to grey with the head tucked and the eye closed.

Each section has a switcher for the large stage (200px plus a 32px overlay pill on light and dark), and a strip below it shows every state at 64px and 32px. The 32px renders use heavier strokes for optical sizing. Under reduced motion every state holds a still pose that is still distinct: an asterisk or traced outline for working, sparks and a sea-glass collar for progress, an amber collar for a nudge.
