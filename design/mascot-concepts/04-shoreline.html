Shoreline: the plover is the progress bar

Mascot concept 04 · three variants · drag the slider to scrub progress

Plover's pitch is visible progress, so in this direction the character does not sit next to the progress UI: it is the progress UI. Each variant borrows one true plover trait (the black breast-band, the run-and-stop dash along the waterline, the round little body) and turns it into a gauge that fills from 0–100%, so a glance at the overlay at 32px reads as "how far along am I" before it reads as "cute bird". Motion stays small and tidal: calm breathing when idle, short bursts while working, one hop per finished subtask. Nudges turn amber and glance at you rather than flashing red. Paused always means three things at once: closed eye, a grey gauge, and a pause badge, so privacy status never depends on motion alone.
