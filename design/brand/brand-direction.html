Plover brand direction, draft 1

Quiet company. Plover is a small ground bird that doesn't fly off or flutter about. It settles near you, the way little birds hop around close to people, and quietly watches while you work. Calm, close and on your side. Everything in the brand follows that: soft round corners, a calm palette, and a voice that coaches gently, never pushes.

Palette: four colours, no pure white or black

Sage #5E7F6B
Brand colour. App icon, buttons, chips, key surfaces.
Cream #F3EEE1
Our "white". Backgrounds, text on sage or ink.
Ink #2F4236
Our "black". A deep green, not black. All body text.
Sand #D9CFB4
Secondary surfaces, dividers, chat bubbles, quiet details.
Beak amber #E3B55B is reserved for the bird's beak only. It never appears in UI, text or other graphics.

Contrast rules: ink on cream 9.3:1 (all body text) · cream on sage 3.8:1 (large/bold text only, 18 px+ bold) · sage text on cream 3.8:1 (headings only) · ink on sage 2.4:1 (never for text).
