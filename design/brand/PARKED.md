# Parked brand ideas

## Closed eye ◡ as a standalone symbol (parked 2026-10-03)
Explored using the bird's closed eye on its own as a small brand mark: a "thinking" loader (sage circle, eye breathing
open/shut), a "done" mark (happy eye ◠ instead of a check), and a sign-off on messages / empty states.
See `brand-direction.html` (draft 1 section) and `loader-test.html` (variant A).

Why parked: on its own the eye reads as a generic smiley, not as Plover; people wouldn't know what it represents.
Decision for now: the loader uses the **whole bird** (head + beak, blinking), the done mark is a **normal check**, and
the eye only ever appears on the bird. Revisit later if the brand gets well known enough for a fragment to carry it.
