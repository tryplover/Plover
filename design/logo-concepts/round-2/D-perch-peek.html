D · Perched peek

Rationale. Instead of rising from below, the bird sits on top of the screen: a cream round body on the upper edge of the tile, two amber feet gripping the lip, head leaning down and in with the beak pointing down-left and one calm closed eye. It reads as the buddy that sits on top of your monitor, watching over you, which fits the real product (an always-on-top window at the edge of the screen) more literally than a bird poking out of a hole. To keep the cream bird legible, the tile is split into a dark ink “sky” above a sage “screen” below; the ledge between them is the perch and the lean toward the screen is the whole emotional gesture. Motion is hop in, settle, lean to look, slow blink, a feather fluff, a sideways hop along the edge, then a hop out.
