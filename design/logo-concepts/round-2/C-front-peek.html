C - Front peek, unmistakably a bird

A front-facing head rises over the bottom edge, but turned about 8 degrees so the beak is a short wedge seen from slightly above and pointing a little left, echoing the side-peek beak. Three plover cues do the species work: a soft sand-toned forehead cap, a dark collar band hugging the edge, and pointed feather-tip wings gripping the edge (no flippers, no chick fluff). Eyes are calm closed arcs at rest and soft pills when alert. The loop is 9 s: rise, wings grip, look left, look right, calm blink, little head bob, sink.
